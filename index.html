CELEBRATIONS OF INDIA

Every festival tells a story.

Explore the colours, customs and happiness of India's festivals.

ALL OVER INDIA

Diwali

The festival of lights, hope and new beginnings.

Discover more ↗

Diwali, the festival of lights, symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. People decorate their homes with diyas, create rangoli, exchange sweets, and celebrate with family and friends.

NORTH INDIA & BEYOND

Holi

A joyful celebration of colours, spring and togetherness.

Discover more ↗

Holi is the festival of colours, celebrated with joy across India. People play with colours, share sweets, and celebrate love, friendship, and the arrival of spring.

KERALA

Onam

A harvest festival celebrated with pookalam, feasts and joy.

Discover more ↗

Onam is a major harvest festival celebrated in Kerala. It welcomes the legendary King Mahabali and features beautiful flower carpets called Pookalam, traditional feasts known as Onam Sadya, boat races, and folk dances.

WEST BENGAL

Durga Puja

A magnificent celebration of art, devotion and community.

Discover more ↗

Durga Puja is one of the most celebrated festivals in West Bengal. It honours Goddess Durga and her victory over evil. Beautiful pandals, artistic idols, cultural performances, and community celebrations make this festival special.
